Our paper **[SentinelKilnDB](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/9ab8bb568825d49ce31aa87b7e2f4ad7-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html)** has been accepted at **NeurIPS 2025** (**Datasets and Benchmarks Track**)! A large-scale dataset of **62,671 hand-validated brick kilns** across South Asia for oriented bounding box kiln detection from satellite imagery, benchmarked against **28+ detection models**. Work from the **[Sustainability Lab](https://sustainability-lab.github.io/)**, IIT Gandhinagar.
