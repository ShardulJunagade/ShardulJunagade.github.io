07_neurips_sentinelkilndb

Created on November 02, 2025

2025

Our paper SentinelKilnDB has been accepted at NeurIPS 2025 (Datasets and Benchmarks Track)! A large-scale dataset of 62,671 hand-validated brick kilns across South Asia for oriented bounding box kiln detection from satellite imagery, benchmarked against 28+ detection models. Work from the Sustainability Lab, IIT Gandhinagar.
